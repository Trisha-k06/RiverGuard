    Methodology

    Pollutant mass entering the mixing point must equal pollutant mass leaving it. RiverGuard adds the load carried by the river to the load carried by the wastewater, then divides by the combined flow.

    Ceffluent = Craw × (1 − η / 100)

    Cdownstream = (Qriver × Cupstream + Qwaste × Ceffluent) / (Qriver + Qwaste)

    Units check: flow (m³/s) × concentration (mg/L) gives a pollutant load in g/s. Dividing by total flow returns a concentration in mg/L.

    Symbols

    Qriver
    River flow upstream of the outfall (m³/s)
    Qwaste
    Wastewater discharge flow (m³/s)
    Cupstream
    Baseline concentration in the river (mg/L)
    Craw
    Pollutant in wastewater before treatment (mg/L)
    η
    Treatment efficiency, the percent of pollutant removed
    Ceffluent
    Pollutant in wastewater after treatment (mg/L)

    Assumptions

    • Steady flow: river flow, discharge and concentrations stay constant over time.
    • Complete mixing: the discharge blends instantly and evenly across the whole river.
    • No decay: the pollutant is conservative, so it does not settle, degrade or react.
    • One discharge: there is a single outfall, with no other inflows or withdrawals.

    Limitations

    • It cannot show near-field effects. Close to a real outfall, concentrations can be far higher than the mixed value.
    • It ignores seasonal low flows, shock loads and changes in treatment performance.
    • It does not apply to pollutants that react or decay, such as BOD, or to dissolved oxygen, which need other models.
    • Results are for learning and early screening. They do not assess legal compliance or ecological harm.

    Where this fits in an EIA

    1. Set the baselineEstablish existing river quality: Cupstream.
    2. Predict the impactEstimate the change with no mitigation: the untreated result.
    3. Test mitigationApply treatment and recalculate: the treated result.
    4. Assess significanceCompare with the baseline and applicable standards. Real projects need field data and regulatory guidance.
